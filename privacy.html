Policy

Privacy policy

Effective August 31, 2026.

What we collect

  • Newsletter signups: your email address, used only to send The Dry Batch newsletter. Unsubscribe links are in every issue. We never sell or share subscriber lists.
  • Contact form: the name, email, and message you send us, used only to reply.
  • Hosting logs: our host (Netlify) processes standard server logs (IP address, user agent) to serve the site.

What stays on your device

The Batch Log tool stores your batch records in your own browser's local storage. That data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use the CSV export for backups.

Tracking

We do not run third-party advertising trackers. If we add analytics, we will use a privacy-respecting provider and update this policy first.

Contact

Privacy questions: use the contact page.
