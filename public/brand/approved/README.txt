SUBLIME PANTRY — LOGO KIT
The sublimation palette. Both marks, one family.

Typeface: Bodoni Moda (SIL OFL — free to use on the site too).
All letters are converted to OUTLINES, not live text. No font has
to be installed anywhere and nothing reflows.

--------------------------------------------------
THE PALETTE
--------------------------------------------------
Ink        #000000   the ground, the solid state
White      #FFFFFF   the crystal
Paper      #FAF8F4   warm off-white for print grounds
Ice        #B4D9EC   what leaves — the vapour
Deep Ice   #7FB6D2   ice for light grounds where the pale one dies
Rust       #8B4A21   the heat that drives it off

The offset ghost is the brand's signature: a second copy of the
mark sitting 3.5% of the type size to the LEFT, in ice or rust.
The mark is always the solid; the ghost is always what is leaving.

--------------------------------------------------
COLOURWAYS
--------------------------------------------------
ink-ice       ink mark, ice ghost      → paper, white, silver
white-ice     white mark, ice ghost    → black, dark photos
ink-rust      ink mark, rust ghost     → the ice ground
rust-ice      rust mark, ice ghost     → paper, warm applications
solid-ink     one colour               → foil, stamps, embossing
solid-white   one colour               → black, one-colour dark

The monogram also carries white-rust (white mark, rust ghost) for
ice and deep-ice grounds, carried over from the old kit.

Never put a white mark on plain Ice — it disappears. That is what
ink-rust is for.

--------------------------------------------------
ON THE SILVER MYLAR BAG
--------------------------------------------------
Use 01-wordmark/sublimepantry-inline-solid-ink.svg, or the tagline
version. One spot colour, solid black. No white underbase, no
halftone, no gradient.

The ice blue is a second colour and it washes out against silver.
Keep the ghost off foil entirely.

--------------------------------------------------
RULES
--------------------------------------------------
Clear space: the height of the crystal (the dot on the i) on all
four sides of the wordmark; the height of the S on the monogram.
It is already built into the files. Do not crop tighter.

Minimum width: wordmark 1 inch / 25 mm in print, 120 px on screen.
Below that use the monogram.

Never add a space between the words, re-space the letters, outline
the mark, add a drop shadow, mirror the ghost to the right, put the
ghost on both sides, or reset any of it in another font. The
weight-and-italic break and the left-hand ghost are the logo.

--------------------------------------------------
NOTE ON THE MONOGRAM
--------------------------------------------------
The SP has been re-cut in Bodoni Moda so it belongs to the same
family as the wordmark. It is close to the old one in weight and
contrast but the letterforms are not identical — the old SP was set
in a different Didone. If you want the original outlines kept,
send the vector and it can be swapped back in without changing
anything else in this kit.
