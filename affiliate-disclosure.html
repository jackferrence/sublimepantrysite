Policy

Affiliate disclosure

As of August 31, 2026, Sublime Pantry has no affiliate relationships, sponsorships, or paid placements. No link on this site earns us money.

What will change, and how you'll know

We expect to add affiliate relationships in the future — most likely for freeze dryers and packaging supplies. When we do:

  • Affiliate links will be marked with rel="sponsored" and disclosed clearly on the page and near the link, not only here.
  • A commercial relationship will never change a comparison's criteria or conclusions; the criteria are published before the products are scored.
  • If we ever sell our own product in a category we cover, that ownership will be disclosed prominently on every page that mentions it.

This approach follows the FTC's Endorsement Guides, which require material connections to be disclosed clearly and conspicuously near the recommendation. We also do not publish fake or AI-generated reviews or testimonials — the FTC prohibits them, and so do our editorial standards.
